PriceGuard

Bought something and the price dropped a week later? Card issuers used to refund the difference, then most dropped the benefit because checking a claim took a person, a receipt and a web page. PriceGuard turns that check into one GenLayer round: validators read the product page, agree on today's price to the cent, and the drop is paid from the issuer's pool.

How a check is decided

1 · The issuer registers the purchase

It has seen the receipt: buyer, product page, item, variant, price paid, date. Only pages on the retail sites the plan covers.

2 · Validators read today's price

Each fetches the page and reports the selling price of that item and variant: not the "was" price, not another size, not an add-on, member or financing price. Code then requires that exact price to be printed on its own copy with the currency sign. They must agree on {status, price_cents} exactly.

3 · Plain code pays the drop

coverage × (paid − lowest price seen), above the plan's minimum, under its cap, minus what was already paid. A deeper drop later pays the difference; the same drop never pays twice. Out of stock, unclear or unreadable pays nothing.
